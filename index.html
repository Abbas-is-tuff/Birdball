How to play

You control the bird with the yellow arrow. Score in the goal on the right!

📱 Touch (iPad)

Left side: drag anywhere to move

SHOOT – hold longer for a harder shot.

TACKLE – slide in to win the ball. Miss and your bird stumbles for a moment!

PASS – passes where you point. The teammate with the blue ring is who gets it.

DRIBBLE – skill move! A quick spin and burst with the ball. Nobody can tackle you during it.

SWITCH – control a different bird (the one with the ball, or the one closest to it).

FLAP – jump! Tap again in the air to fly higher for headers.

SPRINT – hold to run faster (uses stamina).

🎥 (top right) – switch camera: Wide, Full pitch or Close.

⌨️ Keyboard

WASD / arrows – move

Space – shoot (hold)

E – pass · R – switch bird · F – dribble · T – tackle

Q – flap (jump & fly)

Shift – sprint · C – camera · P – pause

⚽ Real football rules

7 birds a side. The ball can go out: throw-ins, corners and goal kicks. At a set piece press PASS or SHOOT (SHOOT = long throw / cross).

Tackle from behind or miss with a slide and it can be a foul: free kick, a 🟨 yellow card, or a PENALTY in the box. Two yellows = 🟥 sent off!

🚩 Offside: don't be behind the last defender when a teammate passes to you.

Birds get tired during the match – pause ⏸ and use 🔁 Substitutions. Watch out for rain: the ball skids!

🐦 Bird tips

Every bird has its own Speed, Shot and Stamina.

Cookie is the fastest bird on the pitch… but gets tired super quickly!

Big birds make great keepers. Tiny birds are quick and never get tired.

Point your joystick at a corner when you shoot to aim past the keeper.
